# Research notice

Spurwerk 0.2 is a research prototype by Dmitrii Shchukin.

The repository does not contain a full Paul Celan corpus or copies of the research sources. Aggregated measurements, limited contextual word profiles, typed interpretive claims, and irreversible source n-gram hashes were derived locally from materials supplied by the project owner. Paraphrased claims retain source identifiers; external full texts are not bundled.
